CTRL + shift + p = open command palette

Vseobecne nastavenia VSCode = settings.json:
    - pacol som tam "files.eol": "\n" aby defaultne pouzival LF ako znak konca riadku
    - pacol som tam "files.encoding": "utf8" aby pouzival utf8 encoding

Pozor na case-insensitive thing on Windows and MacOS and case-sensitive thing on Linux.
